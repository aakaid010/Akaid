About

I completed my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at the Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) in July 2026, with a CGPA of 3.55/4.00 and a final four-semester average of 3.80/4.00. My thesis, Predictive Modeling of Nanocellulose Characteristics from Acid Hydrolysis Process Parameters Using Machine Learning, built a leakage-safe pipeline on a 341-record dataset curated from 96 publications, and showed how far conventional random cross-validation overstates generalisation on literature data.

Alongside the thesis I have been carrying out independent research in prognostics and health management. I designed a stacking ensemble for remaining useful life (RUL) prediction on the NASA C-MAPSS turbofan data, introduced quantitative metrics for how well the explanations of different models agree, and I am now developing an uncertainty-quantified explainable deep learning framework for tool wear prediction in high-speed CNC milling. Since September 2026 I have also been a research intern at ELITE Research Lab.

My broader interest is in predictive maintenance and industrial reliability: models that are validated without leakage, that report their uncertainty, and whose explanations hold up across model families. I am open to research collaborations and graduate study opportunities in these areas, and I am happy to hear from anyone working on related problems.

Research Interests

Prognostics & Remaining Useful Life Prediction

Ensemble and deep learning models for RUL and tool wear prediction, from turbofan engines (NASA C-MAPSS) to multi-sensor CNC milling, with attention to sensor preprocessing, feature engineering, and evaluation by RMSE and the NASA PHM08 score.

Explainable AI for Reliability

Measuring whether explanations can be trusted: cross-model agreement metrics built on SHAP, leakage-free SHAP-based feature pruning, and SHAP and Integrated Gradients analysis for deep models.

Physics-Informed & Uncertainty-Aware Machine Learning

Quantifying and calibrating prediction uncertainty, using paper-grouped validation and conformal prediction on materials data and multi-sensor deep learning for tool wear, and asking how that uncertainty affects explanation stability and maintenance decisions. I am also interested in physics-informed models for intelligent manufacturing and industrial reliability.
