Independent Researcher: Tool-Wear Prediction & Uncertainty Quantification

2026

Manuscript under review at Machining Science and Technology · Code on GitHub

  • Showed that changing only the calibration cutter, with the fitted model and test predictions held fixed, moved nominal 90% interval coverage from 14.9% to 84.2% (Random Forest) and from 42.7% to 85.7% (CNN–BiLSTM), at the cost of much wider intervals. The interleaved split behind this result was chosen after inspecting test outcomes, and the paper says so.
  • Cutter-held-out MAE on PHM2010 under a chronological split was 22.8 µm (Random Forest) and 31.3 µm (CNN–BiLSTM). Under the interleaved split, a sensor-free cut-index control (21.1 µm) matched the CNN–BiLSTM (20.6 µm), so MAE alone does not show transferable wear information.
  • At a 150 µm threshold, per-cut ROC-AUC exceeded 0.998, yet the first warning on cutter C6 came 29 to 39 cuts after the crossing. Ensemble disagreement did not track error.
  • Evaluated the interval procedure on an independent nine-tool milling dataset: 4.61 µm cutter-macro MAE and 97.3% coverage at nominal 90%.

Independent Researcher: Prognostics & Explainable AI

2025 – 2026

Independent research · Chattogram, Bangladesh

  • Designed a stacking ensemble combining Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost with a Ridge meta-learner for RUL prediction on the NASA C-MAPSS FD001 and FD003 datasets.
  • Developed a predictive modelling pipeline covering sensor-data preprocessing, feature engineering, ensemble learning, model evaluation, and explainability analysis.
  • Used Group K-Fold cross-validation to evaluate generalisation, assessed with RMSE and the NASA PHM08 scoring function.
  • Introduced quantitative cross-model explanation agreement metrics (Feature Agreement, Rank Agreement, Pairwise Rank Agreement, and Spearman correlation) using SHAP TreeExplainer.
  • Performed leakage-free SHAP-based feature pruning to investigate the trade-off between predictive accuracy, feature dimensionality, and inference efficiency.
  • Best single learners reached test RMSE 17.57 (FD001) and 19.39 (FD003); stacking did not improve on them, and mean pairwise-rank agreement of 0.87 and 0.84 between models is consistent with largely overlapping signal.
  • With pruning, 5 of 51 features gave RMSE 17.30 against 17.57 for the full set on FD001; FD003 needed about 30 features.
